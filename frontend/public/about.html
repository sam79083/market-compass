About the project

A plain-language dip check for the U.S. stock market.

Market Compass shows four public readings of how stressed the S&P 500 or the NASDAQ is right now, each in plain words, so you can tell a normal dip from calm markets or from a deep sell-off.

Four readings, no score

Panic level (VIX or VXN), an oversold check (RSI), the 200-day trend, and the CNN Fear & Greed index. Each one gets a state like “Calm” or “Pullback” instead of a number you have to decode.

Honest about overlap

These readings usually move together, so four of them agreeing is not four separate confirmations. The page says so.

Not a signal service

It describes current conditions. It does not tell you to buy or sell, and it cannot tell you where the bottom is.

What the page shows

For each of the S&P 500 and the NASDAQ, the page shows how stressed the market is on four measures. Each card has a short state label, a one-to-three dot meter for how much the reading looks like a dip, and a one-sentence reason. Click a card to see its history chart. The thresholds behind each label are listed on the methodology page.

Data and refresh schedule

The site is a static snapshot. A scheduled GitHub Actions workflow refreshes the data on weekday evenings (U.S. market days) and publishes new JSON files. The page you are reading is served from the same static build.

  • VIX: Cboe Global Markets. VXN (NASDAQ-100 volatility): Cboe, via Yahoo Finance.
  • RSI and the 200-day trend: calculated from daily closes of the S&P 500 and the NASDAQ Composite, from Yahoo Finance’s public chart interface.
  • Fear & Greed: the CNN Business Fear & Greed Index.

Some feeds are unofficial and can be delayed or briefly unavailable. When a reading is missing the page says so instead of substituting another metric. Check the “as of” date on each card.

Who it is for

Individual investors, students, and anyone who wants a quick, plain-language read on market stress. It is not a substitute for a financial adviser or your own risk tolerance, and it is not meant for trading on real-time or licensed data. See the terms and disclaimer.

Independence and funding

Market Compass is independently run. There are no sponsored rankings, paid placements, or affiliate recommendations. The site may display advertising to cover domain and hosting costs. Advertising never influences what the readings say.
