Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information is processed when you visit Market Compass, why it is processed, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 2026-11-03 · Contact: info@samori.tistory.com

1. Summary

  • There are no accounts, logins, comments, or user profiles on this site.
  • The site stores your light/dark theme preference in your browser’s local storage.
  • Hosting and security logs are processed by Cloudflare.
  • If advertising is enabled, Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies, subject to your consent where required.
  • You can use the site without accepting personalized advertising; non-personalized ads may still be shown where permitted.

2. Information we process

Information you provide

If you email the contact address, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This is used only to respond and keep a record of corrections or requests.

Information processed automatically

Like most websites, the hosting provider processes technical request data such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring page, requested URL, and timestamps. This data is used to deliver the site, prevent abuse, and maintain security. Cloudflare may process this data as a hosting and security provider.

Local storage

The dashboard stores a single preference key (market-compass-theme) in your browser’s local storage so the light or dark theme persists between visits. It is not sent to a server by this site. You can clear it through your browser settings.

This site does not set its own analytics or tracking cookies. If advertising is enabled, third-party advertising partners may set cookies or use similar technologies to measure ads and, where you consent, personalize them.

Google AdSense. When enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

EEA, UK, and Switzerland. Where required, a Google-certified consent management platform is used to request consent before personalized advertising cookies are set. You can change or withdraw your choice through the consent message or your browser settings. Refusing consent does not prevent you from using the site.

Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control. The site does not currently respond to browser Do Not Track signals. Where required by law, opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control are honored by the advertising consent flow.

4. Analytics

The site may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-focused measurement service that does not use cookies or collect personal information, to understand aggregate traffic. If analytics is enabled, the privacy policy will be updated to name the provider. No cross-site behavioral profiles are created by this site’s own analytics.
